SLAPFISH

Grab a rod. Cast off the pier. Every fish is a weapon.
WASD move   Mouse aim   Click attack   (on a phone: left stick moves, right stick aims and fires)
R or Right click cast, then again to reel in
Space dash (also bails out of fishing)   E swap fish
Leave the line in longer for rarer fish. Pier tips are deep water: faster depth, but nowhere to run. Getting hit snaps your line.
